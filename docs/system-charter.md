# System Charter: Project Enki

## Mission

Build a zero-budget governed knowledge coherence engine that preserves, validates, compounds, and responsibly applies professional and organizational knowledge across Person-Objects and linked contexts.

The engine exists to support:

- executive career opportunity generation through governed downstream products
- knowledge synthesis and longitudinal reconciliation
- evidence-backed intelligence without universal maturity scores
- long-term intellectual property creation
- repeatable knowledge governance
- adaptive learning without unmanaged drift

## Vision

Project Enki begins as a coherence kernel and governance architecture. Its long-term function is to preserve, classify, validate, reconcile, evolve, and apply knowledge across domains while maintaining traceable ancestry from observation to framework.

Media Blitz and Golden-Nibiru are downstream consumers that use Enki-governed knowledge for product-specific publishing, career-opportunity, and placement workflows.

## Core Distinction

This is not a content calendar, job-placement workflow, or other downstream product.

This is a governed system for converting experience, evidence, observation, and source artifacts into increasingly coherent, transferable, and reusable knowledge.

Portable knowledge ownership is determined by provenance, authority, lifecycle, semantic identity, and reuse rather than by topic or by which consumer first encounters it. Product-specific workflows and decisions remain with their consumers. The governing boundary is defined by `docs/architecture-decisions/ADR-0003-portable-knowledge-ownership-and-consumer-boundary.md`.

## Source of Truth Model

Google Drive is the working environment for drafts, spreadsheets, living editorial operations, and active documents.

GitHub is the version-controlled architecture repository for schemas, protocols, templates, governance logic, canonical records, and evidence-backed state.

Conversation and project-folder design streams may contain later architectural evidence. They are not automatically canonical, but material conflict between later design evidence and repository authority must be reconciled before architecture or schema freeze.

## Governing Principle

Enki preserves the distinction between historical truth and current authority. It records where knowledge originated, when it was valid, what changed, why it changed, and which interpretation presently governs downstream use.

That principle applies to architecture itself: older architectural records remain historically true, while explicit later decisions may supersede their governing interpretation.

Downstream products consume governed knowledge. They do not define or replace the Enki core, and projection does not transfer canonical mutation authority.

## Success Measures

Primary success signals include:

- provenance completeness
- authority clarity
- reconstruction fidelity
- controlled knowledge evolution
- framework reuse
- evidence accumulation
- portable knowledge reuse without consumer-owned duplication
- explicit conflict and uncertainty state
- reduced decision and publishing friction
- increased clarity of downstream outputs

Product-specific measures, such as recruiter inbound quality, publication conversion, impressions, engagement, fit scores, or application outcomes, remain valid within the products that own them but do not define Enki's core success.

## Failure Modes

The system fails if it becomes:

- unvalidated drift
- unsupported claims
- disconnected assets
- overwritten history
- ambiguous authority
- a manual copy-paste burden
- a replacement for judgment rather than an amplifier of it
- a downstream product masquerading as the parent knowledge system
- a set of consumer-owned knowledge silos created merely because facts originated in different domains
- a repository whose stale canonical text silently overrides a later accepted architecture decision

## Strategic Constraint

Present budget is `$0.00` plus already available tools and free-tier platforms. This constraint is treated as an architectural feature, not a deficiency.
